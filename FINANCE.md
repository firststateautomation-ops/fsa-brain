## AUDIT — WHAT IT DELIVERS
The FSA onboarding audit is a standardized, highly automated process that produces a deliverable the client can act on immediately — with or without FSA.

Every audit includes:
- Current workflow mapping (where time and money are being lost)
- Top 3–5 automation opportunities, ranked by ROI
- Cost and timeline estimate for each opportunity
- Quick wins the client can implement themselves right now
- A clear picture of what FSA would build and what it would cost

**The audit is the springboard.** It builds trust, demonstrates expertise, and makes the retainer the obvious next step — without requiring a hard sell.

## AUDIT — PROCESS (STANDARDIZED)
1. Client pays $999 — work begins only after payment received
2. Send pre-audit questionnaire (automated via Make.com + Tally)
3. 30-minute discovery call — recorded, transcribed automatically
4. Claude generates audit report from questionnaire + call transcript
5. Bo reviews, refines, and approves report (target: under 1 hour of Bo's time)
6. Deliver report within 5 business days of discovery call
7. Follow-up call to walk through findings and present retainer options
