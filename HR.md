## DELEGATION HIERARCHY
When a task needs to leave Bo's plate, follow this order — no skipping steps:

1. **Claude (Cowork / Claude Code)** — writing, strategy, code, prompts, reports, research
2. **Make.com automation** — repeatable, trigger-based tasks (form handling, notifications, CRM updates)
3. **Specialized AI agent** — tasks requiring ongoing autonomy (monitoring, scheduling, follow-ups)
4. **Human contractor** — only if the task genuinely cannot be handled by steps 1–3

If you're considering hiring a human, ask first: *can an AI agent do this with the right prompt and the right tools?* The answer is usually yes.

## AI AGENT HIRING — HOW IT WORKS
Hiring an AI agent at FSA means:
- Defining the task scope clearly (one job per agent)
- Writing the system prompt / skill file that governs its behavior
- Connecting it to the right tools (Make.com, Tally, CRM, email)
- Testing it on real inputs before it runs unsupervised
- Reviewing its output on a defined cadence — not micromanaging, but not ignoring

**Every agent gets a skill file.** The skill file is its job description, its constraints, and its improvement log — all in one. Stored in fsa-brain.

## ITERATIVE AGENT ENGINEERING
AI agents improve through iteration, not replacement. After every session:
1. Identify what the agent got wrong or could do better
2. Update the relevant skill file with the fix
3. Commit to fsa-brain with a one-line note on what changed and why

This is the compounding advantage — every session makes the next one faster and more accurate. Treat skill files like employees who get better with every shift.

## HUMAN HIRING PHILOSOPHY
- Hire to remove a specific bottleneck that AI genuinely cannot handle
- Never hire before the process is documented — you'll just be managing confusion
- Contractors first, employees never (until revenue is consistent and the role is proven)
- The right first human hire is whoever frees up the most of Bo's billable or revenue-generating time

## FIRST HUMAN HIRE TRIGGER
Do not engage any recurring human contractor until FSA has:
- [ ] 2+ active paying retainer clients
- [ ] A documented, repeatable process for the role being hired
- [ ] Exhausted AI agent options for the role
- [ ] Enough monthly revenue to cover contractor cost and still profit

**Likely first human contractor need:** Client-facing communication support — someone to handle calls and scheduling that require a real voice.

---

## HUMAN CONTRACTOR RULES
- Scope in writing before any work begins — no verbal agreements
- Fixed price or capped hours per engagement — no open-ended retainers
- Pay on delivery of agreed output, not on time spent
- 30-day trial before committing to ongoing work
- Never give contractor access to client data, CRM, or financial accounts without explicit need

## ONBOARDING A NEW CLIENT (FSA's Internal Process)
When a new client signs:

1. Send audit invoice ($999) — work begins only after payment clears
2. Send pre-audit questionnaire via Tally (automated)
3. Schedule 30-minute discovery call — record and auto-transcribe
4. Generate audit report using Claude from questionnaire + transcript
5. Bo reviews and approves report (target: under 1 hour)
6. Deliver report within 5 business days of discovery call
7. Follow-up call to walk through findings and present retainer options
8. On retainer agreement: send Month 1 invoice, begin build

**Target: zero manual admin steps by the time FSA has 5 active clients.**
