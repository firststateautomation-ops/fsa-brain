## TARGET BUYER
- Who: Trades business owner, Dover/Kent/New Castle/Sussex counties
- Business size: 1-10 techs, $300k-$1M revenue
- Pain: Drowning in admin, losing leads after hours, no visibility into business performance
- Language they use: "too busy", "falling through the cracks", "can't keep up"
- What they want: Time back. Money back. Less chaos.
- What they do NOT want: Technology explanations, jargon, or a sales pitch

---

## MESSAGING RULES — NEVER VIOLATE THESE
- Never use the word "automation" in headlines or CTAs
- Never lead with technology — always lead with the outcome
- Time saved and money recovered are the only metrics that matter to this buyer
- Position Bo as strategist first, builder second
- Every CTA is a "strategy call" — never a "workflow audit" or "demo"
- Speak like a trades owner, not a tech company

---

## VOICE AND TONE
- Direct. No fluff.
- Confident without being arrogant
- Local and personal — Dover-built, Delaware proud
- Outcome-obsessed — every sentence earns its place by pointing to a result
- Never corporate. Never coastal tech. Never buzzword-heavy.

## BLOG/SEO STRATEGY
Target: Two-track keyword approach —
1. Zero-competition long-tail keywords specific to Delaware field service (quick wins, indexing now)
2. Higher-volume keywords trades owners search nationally (longer burn, bigger payoff)

Format: 1,200-2,000 words, one keyword cluster per post, internal links to 2+ existing posts
Structure: Problem → Cost of problem → Solution → Local proof → CTA
CTA on every post: Book a strategy call at firststateautomation.ai
Posting cadence: 1-2 posts per week until 20 posts live
Indexing: Submit each new URL to Google Search Console after deploy
